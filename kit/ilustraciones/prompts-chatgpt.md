# Prompts para las cuatro musas

Se usan en una sola conversación de ChatGPT. Primero el mensaje de estilo, y después un mensaje por musa. Si aparece color o gris, pedir: "Redraw it in pure black ink on a pure white background, no grey, no color."

## Mensaje 1: estilo

I'm going to ask you for four illustrations of single figures, all in exactly the same style. Don't generate anything yet; just reply "ready".

Style: an early 20th-century hand-inked illustration, like a vintage printed card drawn by an expressive, theatrical illustrator. Confident, slightly uneven pen-and-ink contour lines of varying weight, bold dark outlines around every shape, flat areas of solid black ink, a little short parallel hatching for shadow, no gradients, no soft shading, no realism. Simple, expressive faces (a few lines, small dots for eyes, a gentle mouth), slightly oversized expressive hands, theatrical gestures. Drapery is stylized, with long curving fold lines and a few small decorative patterns on the fabric (dots, stars, small leaves), the way a woodblock print or an Arts-and-Crafts illustration would handle cloth. A gentle naive charm: proportions slightly elongated and stylized, never anatomically perfect.

Technical rules for every image: black ink only on a pure white background, high contrast, no color, no grey tones, no paper texture, no border or frame, no text, no signature, no ground, no cast shadow. A single full figure, centered, with a generous empty margin all around, vertical format 2:3.

Pose for all four: the figure floats weightlessly in mid-air, as if dancing slowly. The body forms a soft S-curve, torso turned three-quarters, one knee bent with the foot tucked behind the opposite leg, arms open to the sides with one hand a little higher than the other, head turned over the shoulder. A long scarf of thin fabric wraps once around the body and trails behind in a loose spiral. No wreath around the figure, no oval frame, no animals, no other figures, no landscape, no background elements of any kind.

Create a photorealistic product photo of a plain crew-neck cotton t-shirt in deep indigo navy blue (hex #1b1444), front view, laid flat on a pure light grey (#f2f2f2) surface and photographed from directly above. Soft, diffuse studio light, subtle natural cotton texture and a few soft folds. No model, no hanger, no props, no label, no logo, no text.

Print the attached illustration on the shirt as a single-color screen print, centered on the chest, horizontally centered, with its top edge about 8 cm below the collar and a height of about 30 cm. IMPORTANT: reproduce the attached artwork exactly, line for line. Do not redraw, simplify, restyle or add anything to it, and do not change its proportions. The artwork is dark line work: in the print, only the lines are inked, in off-white light grey (#f2f2f2), and everything that is blank in the drawing is not printed at all, so the shirt's blue shows through the figure. Matte, fully opaque plastisol ink with a very slight texture that follows the weave of the fabric. No gradients, no glow, no shadow around the artwork, no second color.
